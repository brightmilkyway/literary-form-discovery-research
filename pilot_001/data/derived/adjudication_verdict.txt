================================================================================
ADJUDICATION VERDICT — the (2, 73) "snow frame" pair
================================================================================

Question
--------
Unit 2 (paras 5-9: Gabriel at the door, scraping snow from his goloshes, "a
cold, fragrant air from out-of-doors") and unit 73 (paras 442-447: Gretta's
memory of Michael Furey, "the window was so wet I couldn't see", the poor
fellow "shivering" in the garden) are the story's opening and closing
thresholds.  MiniLM flags them as similar; hub correction deflates them.

Is this  (i) a real echo that hub correction over-corrects,
         (ii) a pure hub artifact, or
         (iii) something the encoder cannot really see?

VERDICT: none of the three exactly.  It is a **single-encoder artifact**:
the pair is a genuine property of the MiniLM representation, but it does not
reproduce in a second encoder from a different family, while every other
candidate pair does.  Under the pilot's own rule (a finding that appears in
one method only is a candidate to explain, not a finding), (2,73) is withdrawn.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Evidence
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

TEST A — long-range-restricted percentile (MiniLM, pooled)
  The fair baseline for a claim about a *distant* echo is other distant pairs.
    raw cos        rank   5/741 among gap>=40   (99.3rd percentile)
    hub-corrected  rank  20/741 among gap>=40   (97.3rd percentile)
  -> The pair IS extreme among distant pairs, even after hub correction.
     This part of the evidence supports (i), not (ii).

TEST B — leave-one-out hub correction
    centrality of unit 2 : 0.5518 (leave-one-out) vs 0.5540 (full)
    centrality of unit 73: 0.5480 (leave-one-out) vs 0.5502 (full)
    residual             : +0.1168 (leave-one-out) vs +0.1125 (full)
    rank among gap>=40   : 17/741 (leave-one-out) vs 20/741 (full)
  -> Self-inflation is negligible; the hub correction is legitimate.
  -> Diagnostic: units 2 and 73 are *hubs*, not peripheries
     (centrality ranks 73 and 70 of 78; 78 = most central).  The correction
     is therefore doing the right thing here.

TEST C — cross-chunking replication (MiniLM, 100- and 80-word units)
  The *passage block* paras 5-9 x paras 442-447 recurs at the 96th-98th
  percentile of all early x late pairs under both re-chunkings.
  -> Supports (i) *within the MiniLM representation*.

TEST D — cross-model replication  ***DECISIVE***
  Second encoder: BAAI/bge-small-en-v1.5 (different family, 512-token context,
  pooled encoding, 0/156 sub-chunks over the limit).
  Matrix agreement with MiniLM: +0.671 (upper-triangle Pearson).

  Does bge replicate the pairs we call robust?
    (30,39) religion   : hub rank   1/741 -> replicated
    (55,58) song       : hub rank   4/741 -> replicated
    (12,16) Mr Browne  : hub rank   1/741 -> replicated
    (71,74) Gabriel/.. : hub rank   1/741 -> replicated
    (2,73)  snow       : hub rank 282/741, raw 457/741 -> NOT replicated

  Top-20 hub-corrected distant pairs overlap between the two models: 9/20.

  -> bge is not a weaker model; it reproduces every other finding strongly.
     Its rejection of (2,73) is *specific*.

TEST E — correction-free mutual-rank test  ***DECISIVE***
  For a pair (i,j), how highly does each unit rank the other inside its own
  row?  No hub correction involved.  Null (1000 random pairs):
     MiniLM: median 48, 1st pct 2, 5th pct 7
     bge   : median 51, 1st pct 3, 5th pct 10
  "worse of the two ranks" (lower = more convincing):

     pair        MiniLM   bge     verdict
     (55,58) song      1      2    replicated, both models excellent
     (71,74)           2      9    replicated
     (12,16)           5      4    replicated
     (69,72)           7      8    replicated
     (30,39)          11      2    replicated (bge very strong)
     (37,58)           5     33    partial
     (2,73)  snow      5     55    NOT replicated (55 ~ null median 51)

  -> (2,73) is a mutual near-neighbour in MiniLM but indistinguishable from a
     random pair in bge.  Verdict: MiniLM-specific.

TEST F — content-word ablation (reported as a FAILED control)
  Replacing every content word in both passages with a placeholder:
     (2,73)  cos 0.719 -> 0.843      (30,39) cos 0.443 -> 0.865
     null mean over 60 random distant pairs: cos 0.493 -> 0.828
  Two conclusions:

  1. The intended metric ("drop") is CIRCULAR: because every pair converges to
     the same post-ablation level (~0.83), drop ~ 0.83 - cos_before, i.e. it
     merely re-expresses the raw cosine.  This control therefore CANNOT
     adjudicate individual pairs, and is reported as failed.

  2. It nevertheless yields a real methodological finding: MiniLM's cosine
     between same-length prose passages has a FORM BASELINE of about 0.83
     (function words, syntax, punctuation, dialogue layout, length).  All
     observed values (0.44-0.76) lie BELOW that baseline, so absolute cosine
     values must not be read as semantic similarity without subtracting the
     form baseline.  Ratio to the form baseline:
        (2,73) 0.85 (most form-like) | (71,74) 0.84 | (55,58) 0.79
        (12,16) 0.76 | (69,72) 0.74 | (37,58) 0.66 | (30,39) 0.51
        null   0.60

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Consequences for the candidate list
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

WITHDRAWN
  * (2,73) "snow frame" — single-encoder artifact.  Its MiniLM evidence is
    real but not reproducible; its content-ablation profile is the most
    form-like of all candidates.

CORRECTED (this adjudication found an error in the v2 draft)
  * Monastery peripherality (unit 39): MiniLM rank 1/78, but bge rank 21/78.
    The v2 claim that it was "robust across all settings" was based on three
    *MiniLM* settings; it does NOT survive a second model.  Withdrawn as a
    cross-model claim; retained only as a MiniLM observation.

STRENGTHENED
  * (55,58) song lyric x song name — mutual rank 1 and 2, the single most
    robust pair in the whole pilot, in BOTH models.
  * (30,39) religion / Church thread — mutual rank 2 in bge; note that in
    MiniLM its hub-corrected "rank 1" was inflated by the correction because
    both units are peripheral (centrality ranks 5 and 1 of 78).  The
    correction-free mutual-rank test in bge is what makes it credible.
  * (12,16) Mr Browne & Freddy; (69,72); (71,74) — all replicated.
  * Final snow passage (unit 77) peripherality — MiniLM rank 2/78, bge rank
    1/78.  Replicates, unlike the monastery.
  * Units 16 and 30 (the two Mr Browne scenes) are peripheral in both models
    (ranks 3/3 and 5/6).

METHODOLOGICAL ADDITIONS
  * Hub correction has a symmetric flaw: it inflates pairs between two
    PERIPHERAL units.  Any "top hub-corrected pair" list must be accompanied
    by a correction-free check (mutual rank) or by reporting the centrality
    ranks of both units.
  * MiniLM cosine has a form baseline ~0.83 on same-length prose; absolute
    values are not semantic similarity.
