FORM vs CONTENT — corrected design (delete, do not replace)
==============================================================================

  form-only    = passage with every content word DELETED
  content-only = passage with every function word DELETED
  No placeholder token is introduced, so identical tokens cannot
  inflate the similarity.

  WHICH COMPONENT PREDICTS THE ORIGINAL COSINE?
     r(cos_orig, cos_form)    = +0.180
     r(cos_orig, cos_content) = +0.782
     r(cos_form, cos_content) = +0.126

  The withdrawn claim was that a 'form baseline' of about 0.83 sits
  above every observed cosine.  Under the corrected design the
  form-only cosines average 0.712 (sd 0.074) and the
  content-only cosines average 0.413 (sd 0.106),
  so the two components are NOT separable by a single threshold; what
  matters is which one the original cosine tracks (see the
  correlations above).
